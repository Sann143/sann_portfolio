UI/UX Design

Credit Card Repayment

A credit card repayment app is a mobile application designed to assist users in managing and paying off their credit card balances efficiently.

The app includes a loyalty program that lets users earn points for timely payments and repayment milestones.

Montserrat

The Montserrat was selected for its clean, modern appearance and high readability. This font reflects the app’s main goals, with its clean edges and modern shapes aligning well with the point-based system.

Color Palette

Blue was chosen to convey trust and reliability. Yellow is used as an accent color to add energy and highlight key actions.
