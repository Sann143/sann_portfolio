UI/UX Case Study

Advance Salary

Kyo Htote is a Salary Advance Product, providing to customer who receive payroll via MoMoney Corporate Portal.

This product is a financial technology solution that allows employees to access a portion of their earned wages before the traditional payday.

Problem

85% of employees in Asia struggle to cover family expenses before payday.

Insight

Employees frequently request advance salaries to manage urgent needs.

Solution

Created a product called Advance Salary, enabling employees to access their earned wages early, simply, securely, and responsibly.

User Persona

Gathered qualitative insights through user interviews to understand user behaviors, motivations, experiences and the difficulties they face when using the product.

Susan

Junior Staff 20

Susan recently started her first full-time job. She earns a fixed monthly salary and is learning how to manage her finances independently. While her income covers basic needs, unexpected expenses often occur before payday.

Goals
  • Avoid borrowing money from friends or family
  • Access earned salary easily when needed
  • Understand how repayments affect her next salary
Pain Points
  • Salary does not always last until payday
  • Feels uncomfortable requesting advance salary from HR
  • Worried about hidden fees or unclear repayment terms

May

Senior Staff 42

May has been working for over 15 years and is financially responsible for her family. Her salary is stable, but monthly expenses such as children’s education, household costs, and family emergencies often create financial pressure before payday.

Goals
  • Support family needs without financial stress
  • Avoid loans or informal borrowing
  • Maintain financial stability and dignity
Pain Points
  • High monthly family expenses
  • Discomfort requesting advance salary from HR
  • Worried about repayment deadlines and how they affect the next salary

Empathy Map

Ideation

After completing the empathize phase, we gained valuable insights from our target audience and used them to define key problem areas, which we then prioritized into Sprint 1 and Sprint 2 for prototyping and development.

Sprint 1

MVP Release
1. Flexible Repayment Module

Allows employees to manage and customize repayment schedules based on their needs.

2. Seamless Excel Data Import

Enables HR teams to quickly and accurately upload employee financial data using Excel-compatible formats.

Sprint 2

Enhancement Release
1. HR System Integration

Seamlessly connects with existing HR systems for automated data synchronisation and easier management.

2. Enhanced Help & Support Center

Improves user experience with interactive tutorials and step-by-step video guides.

Crafting the Experience

Learn. Test. Improve.

After completing the visual design, I tested the prototype with 8 representative users to see how user friendly the portal is.
